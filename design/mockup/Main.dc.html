4. Port trial program

The CTO presented the proposed hull-inspection trial program across three ports and the draft CRC-P application with the university and Portside Marine.

The board will not fund the multi-port trial program from retained earnings in FY27. A single-port trial at Brisbane remains within the approved FY27 budget.

Resolved: the board endorses the CRC-P application and the company's proposed cash and in-kind contributions, subject to the grant being awarded.

5. Other business

Nil.
