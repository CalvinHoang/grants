# CRC-P Round 19 — program overview

## Objectives (GL §2) — the "policy intent" the application must align to
1. "improve the competitiveness, productivity and sustainability of Australian industries, especially where Australia has a competitive strength, and in line with government priorities"
2. "foster high quality research to solve industry-identified problems through industry-led and outcome-focused collaborative research partnerships between industry entities and research organisations"
3. "encourage and facilitate small and medium enterprise (SME) participation in collaborative research."

## Intended outcomes (GL §2)
Industry–research collaborations established; SME participation in collaborative research; collaborative research results aligned with government priorities; research skills built in industry; improved competitiveness/productivity for participants; stakeholders value the program.

## Round 19 focus (GL §1.1, App A, §8.2)
- All industry sectors, with a focus on **Government priorities** = AI systems/technologies (National AI Plan), National Reconstruction Fund (NRF) priority areas, National Science and Research Priorities, or other Australian Government science/research/industry/innovation priorities.
- **AI Accelerator**: up to $20M for projects that develop or enhance AI systems/technologies in healthcare, agriculture, resources & energy, or advanced manufacturing (National AI Plan Action 2).
- Meritorious applications from eligible **Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander organisations** (ICN or Supply Nation) are prioritised.
- Other meritorious applications capped at ~20% of funding per NRF priority area.
- NRF priority areas: value-add in resources; value-add in agriculture, forestry & fisheries; transport; medical science; renewables & low emission technologies; defence capability; enabling capabilities.
- Science & Research Priorities: (1) net zero; (2) healthy, thriving communities; (3) Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander knowledge systems; (4) protecting & restoring the environment; (5) secure, resilient nation.

## Money (GL §3, App B)
- Grant **$100k – $3M**; "only CRC-Ps of exceptional merit are likely to be awarded the maximum".
- Grant **≤ 50% of total eligible project expenditure** (= eligible expenditure + eligible in-kind). Partners' cash, cash-staff and in-kind treated equally for the matching share.
- Total eligible project value **≥ $200k**. Max project period **3 years**.
- Program total $847M over 2025-26 to 2028-29 across CRCs and CRC-Ps; past CRC-P rounds ~$30–50M.
- Other government grants can count as contributions if their terms allow. Rural RDC discretionary cash counts as cash (see BR-29).
- Funding is indexed; indexation is passed on to grantees in each year's payments (notified in writing before the end of September each year).
- The Minister may award a different amount and term from that requested (FAQ).

## Process (GL §1, §8)
Single-stage application via business.gov.au portal → eligibility screening by department → merit assessment by the **CRC Advisory Committee** (independent experts reporting to Industry Innovation and Science Australia), which may use independent technical experts and other agencies → comparative ranking + "value with money" → due diligence (financial, legal, governance, national interest, national security) → Minister decides (no merit review).
- Assessment is **comparative**: "how well it meets the criteria, how it compares to other applications, whether it provides value with money".
- Tie-breakers: value with money, geographic distribution, program alignment.
- Assessment ~10 weeks after close.

## Post-award (GL §10–12) — useful for advice, not for the application
- Grant agreement (standard terms, not negotiable) executed within 30 days of offer.
- **Partners agreement within 60 days** of execution or commencement of the grant agreement, whichever is later (template available, use not mandatory). The sample grant agreement requires it to bind partners to undertake the project as specified, make their contributions, share information with the lead, make IP ownership clear, and carry equivalent terms (breach, IP, access, fraud, compliance, WHS, acknowledgement, conflicts, records, reporting, privacy, confidentiality, insurance, survival). Variations go to the department within 10 business days.
- Quarterly payments per the agreed schedule, subject to satisfactory progress reports; 5% withheld until a satisfactory end-of-project report; independent financial audit with the end-of-project report (audit cost ≤1% of total eligible project expenditure; auditor must be a registered company auditor, CPA, IPA member or CA ANZ member independent of the grantee).
- Progress, end-of-project and post-project (1 year after) reports.
- Must notify changes incl. new foreign government affiliations. No increase of grant via variation.
- No public announcement before the Minister's; acknowledgement wording "This project received grant funding from the Australian Government."
- Compliance: Australian Code for the Responsible Conduct of Research, National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research, NHMRC/OGTR ethics codes, state/territory working-with-children laws, export controls, AIATSIS Code of Ethics, Traditional and Cultural knowledge and biodiscovery laws, EPBC Act.
