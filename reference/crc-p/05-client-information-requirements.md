# CRC-P Round 19 — information and evidence needed from the client

The basis for gap detection and the RFI template. Party codes: **L** lead SME · **I** second industry partner(s) · **R** research organisation · **All** every partner. "Typical source docs" = what an advisor usually finds in the client folder.

## A. Eligibility and entity data

| Info item | For | Party | Typical source docs |
|---|---|---|---|
| ABN, ACN, legal entity name, GST registration | EL-01/02, AF-F.1 | All | ABN Lookup, ASIC extract |
| Entity type; trust + trustee details | EL-04, EL-12, ATT-01 | L | Constitution, trust deed |
| Headcount; consolidated group membership and group headcount | EL-03, AF-E.1 | L | Payroll summary, group structure chart |
| Latest FY turnover, export revenue (BAS), R&D spend, taxable income, contractors | AF-E.1 | L | BAS, tax return, financial statements |
| ANZSIC division/class | AF-E.2 | L | ABN record |
| Share of revenue from trading (trading corp test); % of funding from government grants | EL-04, EL-12 | L | Financial statements |
| Indigenous ownership/control; ICN or Supply Nation | PR-01, ATT-02 | All | Registry / Supply Nation certificate |
| Partner type and eligibility as "research organisation" / "industry entity" | EL-05 | I, R | Partner details |
| WGEA status (if 100+ staff), Redress Scheme, sanctions exposure | EL-13–15 | All | Declarations |
| Board/authority endorsement | EL-16, ATT-04 | L | Board minute |
| Partner confirmations signed off by authorised rep | EL-18, ATT-05 | All | Email/letter from each partner |

## B. Per merit sub-criterion

| Ref | Information needed | Evidence that persuades (*inferred*) | Party | Typical source docs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MC-1a | The industry problem/opportunity, who identified it, size/cost to the industry; how the project improves competitiveness, productivity, sustainability; which government priorities and why | Market/industry data with sources, customer pain quantified, priority named with mechanism | L, I | Pitch deck, business plan, market research, customer feedback, industry reports |
| MC-1b | How the lead drives the research; research questions; why this research partner; outcome focus | Lead's decision rights, co-design history, prior collaboration outputs | L, R | Proposal, MoUs, prior project reports |
| MC-1c | How SMEs participate and benefit (partners and wider SME base) | Number of SMEs involved/benefiting, adoption pathway | L, I | Supply-chain/customer lists |
| MC-2a | Research plan: work packages, methods, partner roles per WP, milestones, timeline | WP table mapped to partners and milestones | R, L | Research proposal, project plan, Gantt |
| MC-2b | State of the art (national/international), gap, novelty, how it solves the problem, adoption; starting/target TRL with justification | Literature/patent landscape, TRL evidence (prototype, trials) | R, L | Literature review, patent search, test results |
| MC-2c | Students, interns, secondments, industry staff training, workshops (numbers) | Counted outputs with timing | R, All | Research org proposal, HR plans |
| MC-3a | Governance: steering committee, project manager, reporting, decision-making, planning, security arrangements | Named roles and cadence | L | Governance plan, org chart |
| MC-3b | Risk register: technical, commercial, partner, national security, international partners, export control, IP, cyber/data | Specific risks with mitigations | All | Risk register, security plan, IP strategy |
| MC-3c | Named key personnel (≤3 on form) with skills/experience; confirmed funding; facilities, equipment, technology, IP access | CVs summarised to track record; funds confirmed; named facilities | All | CVs, bios, financial statements, equipment lists, IP register |
| MC-4a | Counterfactual without grant; why this amount; effect on scale/timing | Clear alternative scenario, budget justification | L | Board papers, budget |
| MC-4b | Total investment leveraged; why Government should invest; how benefits stay in Australia (jobs, manufacturing, IP ownership) | Leverage ratio, Australian jobs/production numbers | L, I | Financial workbook, business plan |
| MC-4c | Regional/remote sites, customers, suppliers, communities benefiting | Named locations with ASGS remoteness | All | Site list, customer list |
| MC-4d | Commercial outputs (products/processes/services), route to market, target customers, market size, pricing/revenue, spill-overs, post-project plan and funding | Market sizing, LOIs/pilots summarised, commercialisation timeline | L, I | Business plan, commercial model, sales pipeline |
