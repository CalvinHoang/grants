# CRC-P Round 19 — attachments and deliverables

Key finding: **CRC-P requires almost no attachments.** The application is the online form. GL §7.1: "You should only attach requested documents… We will not consider information in attachments that we do not request." Limits: total 50MB, each file <25MB, types docx/rtf/pdf/xlsx/csv/jpg/jpeg/png/gif, filenames letters/numbers only and <40 chars.

## Submitted with the application

| Ref | Attachment | Mandatory? | Trigger | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATT-01 | Trust deed / trust documents showing the incorporated trustee's relationship to the trust | **Mandatory where applicable** | Lead applies as trustee (EL-04b) | GL §4.2, §7.1; AF-L.2 |
| ATT-02 | Indigenous Corporation Number (ICN) or evidence of Supply Nation registration | **Mandatory to claim priority** (optional otherwise) | Lead claims ATSI priority (PR-01) | GL §4.2, §7.1; AF-J.4 |

**Not requested, so do not attach** (content must instead be summarised within the character-limited form fields): letters of support, partner letters of commitment, CVs, business plan, market reports, project plan/Gantt, financial statements, IP searches, security plan.

## Post-award (for the advisor, not the application)
| Ref | Item | When |
|---|---|---|
| ATT-08 | Grant agreement (standard, sample published) | Executed within 30 days of offer |
| ATT-09 | Partners agreement (template published: parties, project activity, contributions table, milestones, partner obligations, duration, reporting, IP (Schedule 1: ownership of project material, publication, utilisation), equipment/assets, confidential info; standard conditions incl. partner removal, which requires assigning its share of Project Material IP) | Within 60 days of execution or commencement of the grant agreement, whichever is later |

## Implication for the tool's deliverables
1. **Application form draft** (all AF fields, within limits) — primary deliverable.
2. **Populated Financial Workbook** / budget tables — second deliverable; source of truth for EL-07–09 and BR checks.
3. **RFI** to the client, split by party (lead, industry partner, research org).
4. Optional advisory outputs: ATT-01/02 checklist, security-plan outline, partners-agreement heads of terms (IP, contributions, governance) — agreed early so the application's IP/governance claims are consistent.
