# CRC-P Round 19 — budget rules (deterministic checks)

Source: GL §3, §5, App B, C, D, E; sample form AF-H; Financial Workbook (ATT-03). Checked against the original guidelines DOCX/PDF on 2026-09-27. Quoted text in "…" is verbatim.

Note: GL App B.1 "How we calculate your total eligible project expenditure" has a heading but **no body text** in both the published PDF and DOCX. The definition comes from GL §3.1: grant "up to 50 per cent of total eligible project expenditure … which includes the eligible expenditure (Appendix C) plus allowable in-kind contributions (Appendix D)".

## Totals, shares and timing

| Ref | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Total eligible project expenditure = eligible expenditure (App C) + allowable in-kind contributions (App D) | §3.1 (App B.1 is blank) |
| BR-02 | Grant ≤ 50% of BR-01; $100,000 ≤ grant ≤ $3,000,000 ("only CRC-Ps of exceptional merit are likely to be awarded the maximum") | §3.1 |
| BR-03 | Partner cash + cash-staff + in-kind ≥ grant; "We treat cash, cash-staff and in-kind contributions equally for determining your minimum matching 50 per cent share" | App B.2; AF-F.2 |
| BR-04 | Total eligible project value ≥ $200,000 | §5.1 |
| BR-05 | Expenditure must be incurred by the lead or partners within the agreement period, be a direct cost of the project or for project audit, and meet the guidance. Project may start from notification of success, but nothing is the Commonwealth's responsibility until the grant agreement is executed; costs before notification are ineligible (BR-30c) | §5.2, App C, App E |
| BR-29 | Rural RDC direct cash from discretionary funds (incl. levies and Commonwealth funds) counts in full as cash. Where an RDC has issued funds to other parties for a specific purpose and those projects are folded into the CRC-P, only the industry-levy component counts, and as in-kind | App B.2 |
| BR-34 | Cash contribution = money "which is not a loan", provided for the project and "immediately available for use"; excludes cash-staff; not reimbursed from grant funds. Cash-staff = money for staff salaries and on-costs, not reimbursed from grant | Glossary; AF-F |
| BR-21 | Duration ≤ 36 months | §3.2 |

## Labour and contractors (App C.3–C.5)

| Ref | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BR-06 | Labour: employees the lead or partners directly employ (paid a regular salary with tax instalments deducted) on core project elements. Max claimable salary for "an employee, director or shareholder, including packaged components" = **$200,000 full-time equivalent per FY**, reduced proportionally for part-years. Excess is paid from own resources and "cannot be reported as eligible expenditure and cannot be claimed as a contribution" | App C.3 |
| BR-06a | Salary = total remuneration package per PAYG annual payment summary; salary-sacrificed super counts only above the Superannuation Guarantee amount | App C.3 |
| BR-06b | Employee time paid for by a partner is that partner's **cash-staff** contribution and must not be reimbursed from grant funds | App C.3 |
| BR-07 | On-costs: eligible salary may be increased by "an additional 30 per cent allowance" (super, payroll tax, workers comp, and overheads such as office rent and computing equipment related to the project), i.e. at most ×1.3 | App C.4 |
| BR-07a | Formula (image in the guidelines; its alt text): "Eligible salary costs equal the annual salary package times the number of weeks spent on the project divided by 52, times the percentage of time spent on the project" (then + up to 30% on-costs) | App C.4 |
| BR-08 | Technical project management ≤ **10%** of total eligible labour claimed; administrative PM ineligible; leadership/admin staff (CEOs, CFOs, accountants, lawyers) ineligible "unless they are carrying technical activities on the core elements of the project" | App C.3 |
| BR-09 | No notional labour: "You cannot calculate labour costs by estimating the employee's worth"; no money exchanged = not eligible | App C.4 |
| BR-10 | Contractors: written contract/letter/PO before any project work, stating the work and fees. Invoices must describe the work, hours and hourly rates, and any plant expenses. Contractors must keep records of their project costs; missing records can make the expense ineligible | App C.5 |
| BR-33 | Labour evidence: name, title, function, time on project and salary for all personnel; ATO payment summaries, pay slips, employment contracts | App C.4 |

## Travel and overseas (App C.6, C.7) — new

| Ref | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BR-13 | Total travel (domestic + overseas) "generally limited to 10 per cent of total eligible project expenditure"; more only if the Program Delegate approves | App C.6, App E; AF-H |
| BR-22 | Domestic travel: "reasonable cost of accommodation and transportation required to conduct agreed project and collaboration activities in Australia" | App C.6 |
| BR-23 | Overseas travel: only where "material to the conduct of the project in Australia", which you must demonstrate; at an economy rate | App C.6 |
| BR-24 | Air fares: economy fare per sector. If a higher class is flown, only the economy-equivalent is eligible, and the grantee needs evidence of the economy fare at the time of travel | App C.6 |
| BR-25 | Overseas expenditure = eligible expenditure incurred outside Australia, **excluding travel**, directly related to the project. Assessed for value for money, considering: share of total grant spent overseas; share of the service provider's fee spent overseas; how it helps meet program objectives | App C.7 |
| BR-12 | Overseas expenditure "generally limited to 10 per cent of total eligible project expenditure"; more only if the Program Delegate approves | App C.7, App E; AF-H |

## Audit (App C.8)

| Ref | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BR-11 | Independent audit costs ≤ **1%** of total eligible project expenditure | App C.8, App E; AF-H |

## In-kind contributions (App D) — new

| Ref | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| BR-26 | In-kind = non-cash contributions (facilities, equipment, services a partner provides from its own resources). To count, they must "directly relate to eligible activities" and be "realistic, justifiable, and valued proportionally to their use on the project". No prescribed formula; the applicant sets the value | App D |
| BR-27 | Employee time is **not** in-kind; it is the partner's cash-staff contribution and eligible expenditure under App C | App D |
| BR-28 | Valuation examples given: capital item = running costs + depreciation (e.g. $100k annual depreciation × 10% capacity = $10k/yr); fee-per-use × uses provided free (e.g. $500 × 100 = $50k/yr); IP = reasonable estimated licensing revenue to the project; office space = cost to rent equivalent space | App D |

## Ineligible expenditure (App E) — new

| BR-30o | Routine operational expenses: communications, accommodation, office computing, printing and stationery, postage, legal and accounting fees, bank charges | App E |

Note the tension an advisor has to resolve with evidence: App C.2 lists "purchase of computing equipment and software" and "reasonable fit out" as eligible, while App E excludes computers, software and renovation "unless directly related to the project". The deciding test is direct relation to the project.

## Financial Workbook (ATT-03) structure

File: `source/crcp-r19-financial-workbook.xlsx` (read 2026-09-27). Three sheets. Recommended, not uploaded; its figures are typed into AF-F.2 and AF-H.

**1. Instructions** — six text blocks: purpose; up to 10 partners (lead + 9), more by copying 8-row partner blocks (totals use SUMIF so they still work, but the Partner Contributions sheet stops at 10); only light-yellow cells are inputs, **whole dollars only**; the Cash-Staff calculator (columns I–M, pale green inputs) is per FY and its result is copied by hand into the yellow cell; the balance checks (below); sheets are meant to stay protected (the downloaded file is actually unprotected).

**2. Budget Worksheet** (A1:AE137)
- Header: Lead Project Partner name (row 2), project title (row 3).
- Grant request, row 7 "Cash", by FY columns **B–F = 2026/27, 2027/28, 2028/29, 2029/30, 2030/31**, G = total.
- 10 partner blocks (rows 11–79, 7 rows apart): partner name; rows Cash / Cash-Staff / In-kind by FY; "Total value of contributions".
- Beside each block, Cash-Staff calculator (I–M): staff member, annual salary, % time, on-costs %, $value = salary × %time × (1 + on-costs%). Examples use on-costs of 15–30%.
- Rows 81–86: totals across partners by type (SUMIF). H86 warns "Partner contributions are less than the grant request" (the BR-03 check).
- Rows 88–94: Grant funds; Partner cash + cash-staff; **Total cash resources**; Non-cash in-kind; **Total project value** (G94 only).
- Rows 96–108: expenditure by FY for 9 heads: Audit costs, Capital, Consumables, Contractors, IP & Technology, Labour including on-costs, Other Expenditure, Overseas, Travel; plus In-kind expenditure (row 107, copied from in-kind contributions); row 108 "Total cash expenditure" = the 9 heads, excluding in-kind.
- Rows 109–111 checks: surplus per FY; UNDER/EQUAL/OVER per FY; cumulative "OK"/"INSUFFICIENT FUNDS" (cumulative spend to each FY end cannot exceed cumulative cash); G111 TRUE when total cash expenditure = total cash resources.
- H/I98, 105, 106: % of total and warnings: audit > 1% "WARNING"; overseas > 10% and travel > 10% "CAUTION … generally limited".
- Rows 115–124: definitions of each head (e.g. Consumables "goods used that must be replaced regularly"; Other = publication/deployment, workshops/conferences/PD/networking for knowledge transfer, project-related staff training, education and training, SME engagement).

**3. Partner Contributions** (A1:E407) — no inputs; mirrors the portal's per-partner screen (Is the party contributing? Yes; Cash / Cash-Staff / In-Kind × FY, Amount, Description) for partners 1–10, pulling from the Budget Worksheet.

Things in the workbook that differ from the guidelines or form (facts, for whoever builds budget checks):
- **G88 (total grant funds) sums B88:E88 and omits 2030/31**, so if grant is budgeted in the fifth FY, "Total cash resources" and the G111 balance check come out wrong.
- The 1%/10% warnings divide by **total cash expenditure** (excludes in-kind), whereas the guidelines set the caps against **total eligible project expenditure** (includes in-kind). The workbook check is therefore stricter than the rule.
- Workbook has 5 FYs (to 2030/31); the sample form shows 4 (2026/27–2029/30) and says the FYs shown follow the project dates.
- The on-cost % in the calculator is free input; the 30% cap (BR-07) is not enforced.
